1.	Organisation Overview
On the 20th of July 2026 I interviewed the 2 owners of Bliss doughnuts to gain more insight on their business operations, objectives and website requirements. This engagement between us provided valuable first-hand information that forms the foundation of the project proposal.

Name: Bliss Doughnuts

Brief History: Bliss Doughnuts was started in 2021 in Johannesburg by 2 friends who wanted to bring joy through gourmet doughnuts. What began as a home bakery has grown into a local favourite with 2 stores.

Mission Statement: To spread happiness one doughnut at a time by creating fresh, fun, and flavourful treats and to deliver authentic, high-quality doughnuts.

Vision statement: To become Johannesburg’s most loved doughnut brand known for creativity, quality and by maintaining excellence in food and service and expanding into nearby communities. 

Target Audience: Students, young adults, families around Johannesburg and event planners looking for desserts and catering.

5.	Design and User Experience

Colour Scheme:
The bliss doughnut website will have a warm and welcoming colour palette that reflects the authentic sweetness taste, with the lovely Pink (#FF69B4) as the primary colour complemented by Cream (#FFF8DC) as the icing on the doughnuts, (Brown #8B4513) for the secondary elements and a charcoal black (#263238) for the text and contrast (ColorHunt, n.d). 

Typography:
The typography system will combine the elegant serif font Playfair Display for headings to convey sophistication and elegance, while showing clean sans-serif fonts like open sans or Roboto for body text to ensure optimal readability across all devices (Googlefonts, n.d).
The design will follow a clear stacked structure from H1 heading down to the bottom text, maintaining consistency throughout the site. The layout will feature a clear gride system that showcases appetising food images sourced from FreePik, n.d followed by a navigation bar for easy access to all sections.

User Experience Considerations:
The experience for the user will prioritize efficiency and simplicity, letting visitors to view the menu and make orders with simple minimal clicks while ensuring sit is fully optimised for mobile devices. There will be high contrast between text and background elements will guarantee readability, while consistent visual branding will reinforce the business’s identity across all pages, creating an engaging digital experience that mirrors the welcoming atmosphere of the actual business live.
