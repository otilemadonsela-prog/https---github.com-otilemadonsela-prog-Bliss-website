Where Bliss Doughnuts Begane

Bliss Doughnuts was started in 2021 in Springs, Johannesburg, South Africa by 2 friends who believed everyone can be excited if they had a doughnut. what began as a small home based business, making doughnuts for friends and family, rapedly grew as word spread about the Scrumptious and delectable taste. Today Bliss Doughnut has to local stores and delivers Scrumptious doughnut to your doorstep across the community wanting to bring joy through gourmet doughnuts.

Meet The Ladys

They are the heart and hands behind every Bliss Doughnut. "the reason why we started this is because we saw a gap in the market and we both believe and know that sweet treats like doughnuts bring people togethere"

Vision

To become Johannesburgs most loved doughnut brand known for creativity, quality and by maintaining excellence in food and service and expanding into nearby communities

Mission

To spread happiness one doughnut at a time by creating fresh, fun, and flavourful treats and to deliver authentic, high-quality doughnuts.
